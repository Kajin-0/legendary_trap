# RoFormer rescue rejection

`modern_separation_rescue_exhausted` for this phase. The preferred Kim
MelBand-RoFormer checkpoint is explicitly noncommercial (`CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`),
and the inspected alternatives do not provide a clearly permissible weight
license. The experiment consequently stopped before inference; it must not be
interpreted as evidence that RoFormer audio separation cannot improve FOCUS.

The next engineering branch should be model adaptation/fine-tuning, a
legally usable source stem, or an explicitly estimated/manual fallback.
