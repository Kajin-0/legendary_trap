# Aesthetic visualizer prototypes

Three deterministic visualizer presets were rendered from the same 18-second
`off_the_wave` interval starting at 20 seconds. Subtitle timing was read from
the existing canonical timing document and was not changed.

| Preset | Geometry | Audio response | Runtime |
|---|---|---|---:|
| orbital | elliptical radial spectrum with dark lyric-safe core and layered depth rings | bass radius/breathing, mids radial displacement, highs outer detail, transients/particles | 38.248 s |
| horizon | mirrored horizontal spectrum with reflected lower layer | bass thickness, mids body, highs edge detail | 31.258 s |
| atmospheric | low-profile spectrum beneath drifting color haze | bass background breathing, mids spectrum, highs particle brightness, transients bloom | 31.938 s |

All previews are 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, and 18 seconds long. The
review package contains the three MP4s and their representative PNG frames.

## Hybrid direction

The follow-up `trap_sunset_hybrid` prototype keeps the same interval and subtitle
policy while adding a procedural dusk landscape, three inertial particle depth
classes, a bass-biased low horizon spectrum, transient impact motion, dynamic
edge vignette, and continuous sunset-to-indigo palette drift. Its preview is
delivered separately in `legendary-trap-aesthetic-previews-v2.zip`.

The refined `v2` preview uses an explicit 18-second palette interpolation from
daylight blue through gold, amber, magenta, and indigo; a dense filled ribbon
with bass-weighted envelope; Lato Semibold centered subtitles; and signed
sub-degree rotation layered onto the transient translation shake.
